Sector Context: AI Infrastructure & Semiconductor Themes

Several macro and sector-wide catalysts are driving broad-based moves across the list today:

ASML / High NA EUV adoption accelerates: ASML secured commitments from both Samsung and TSMC to adopt its High NA EUV lithography tools — Samsung targeting DRAM high-volume manufacturing by 2028, TSMC from 2030. Both companies are joining an industry initiative to develop 12-inch photomasks, which should improve fab productivity and reduce chipmaking costs. BofA reiterated Buy on ASML, calling the photomask initiative the most strategically important announcement.

Philadelphia SOX read-across: The SOX rose 1.3% on Tuesday, driving broad follow-through across Korean and Taiwanese hardware names. Kospi jumped as much as 2.3% in early Wednesday trade, led by SK Hynix (+5% intraday) and Samsung.

AI memory demand confirmation: OpenAI Korea's head expressed confidence that the AI-led memory boom will continue.

Mirae Asset projects DRAM prices to rise 15.8% QoQ in 3Q and 7.2% in 4Q.

Chinese DRAM challenger CXMT raised its global market share to 10% in 2Q, narrowing the gap with the top three.

Macro headwind — oil/rates: Brent crude settled near $98/bbl following Houthi strikes on Saudi energy infrastructure, stoking rate-hike fears. The S&P 500 fell for a second straight session Tuesday; money markets priced >50% probability of a Fed rate increase. This is a cross-cutting headwind for the list.

Apple foldable iPhone launch (today): Apple's Sep 9 "Surprise and Shine" event is expected to unveil the iPhone 18 Pro and its first foldable iPhone (reportedly priced above $2,000), with read-across for Taiwan supply chain names.
