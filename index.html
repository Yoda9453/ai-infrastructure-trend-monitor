Sector / Tape Context (covering the full top 30)

Oil at $100 / rates shock is the dominant macro headwind. Brent crude settled at $101.21 on Sep 9 — its highest close since July — driven by US-Iran military exchanges near the Strait of Hormuz. The 10-year Treasury yield hit its highest since 2023, and the 2-year yield reached its highest since January 2025. The S&P 500 fell 0.5% for a third consecutive session, with industrials the worst sector. This is the primary explanation for the broad selloff in power/cooling names (VRT, ETN, SU, ABBN, MOD) and the general risk-off tone. (1) (2) (3)

Piper Sandler sector initiation reshapes the compute pecking order. Piper initiated NVDA (Overweight, $300 PT), AMD (Overweight, $600 PT), and MRVL (Overweight, $270 PT) with buy ratings, while starting INTC at Neutral ($110 PT). The note frames NVDA and AMD as "long-term winners" in AI compute, and MRVL as "the leader in custom attach." This drove divergent moves within the semiconductor group on Sep 9. (4)

HBM demand broadening is a positive read-across for memory. Mirae Asset raised its SK Hynix target to KRW 3.1 million (from 2.8 million), citing expectations that HBM demand will broaden as hyperscalers deploy proprietary AI accelerators. Micron was cited as a read-across beneficiary. SK Hynix is trading up ~1.6% intraday in Seoul this morning. (5)

Neocloud / hyperscaler capex: Google's €13bn Finland commitment is a positive signal, but execution risk is visible. Google announced at least €13bn in AI infrastructure investment in Finland (2027-28), including three new data centers and a nuclear power purchase agreement with Fortum. Separately, Bloomberg reported that Google and Blackstone's "Crux AI" (Project Braid) neocloud venture is facing delays at major data-center sites. The juxtaposition — massive capex commitment alongside execution friction — is a mixed read for neocloud operators like CRWV and NBIS. (6)

Citi Global TMT Conference (Sep 8-10, New York) is a live catalyst. ALAB, MRVL, NBIS, SMCI, SNDK, and CRWV all presented at the conference within the news window. AMD CFO Jean Hu's TAM comment ($2-3 trillion by 2030) and Marvell CEO Matt Murphy's "Switzerland of AI" positioning were the highest-impact conference moments. (7) (8)

Data center political/regulatory risk is escalating. Massachusetts Governor Healey signed an executive order requiring local approval for new data centers. A GOP senator publicly called for an end to AI data center subsidies. A Bloomberg explainer noted data center backlash has become a defining US midterm election issue. These are emerging headwinds for US-based hyperscaler capex and could affect permitting timelines for EQIX, DLR, and neocloud operators. (9) (10) (11)
